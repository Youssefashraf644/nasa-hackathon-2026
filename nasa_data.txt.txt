The Apollo 11 Lunar Module Eagle was left on the Moon after the first crewed landing in July 1969. The descent stage remains on the lunar surface, while the ascent stage was deliberately crashed into the Moon after docking.

The Spirit rover landed on Mars in January 2004 and became stuck in soft soil in 2009. NASA officially ended communications with Spirit in May 2011. It is now a stationary science platform on Mars.

The Opportunity rover landed on Mars in January 2004 and operated for nearly 15 years. A global dust storm in June 2018 coated its solar panels, and NASA declared the mission complete in February 2019. Opportunity holds the record for the longest distance driven on another world.

The Lunar Crater Observation and Sensing Satellite, or LCROSS, deliberately impacted the Moon's Cabeus crater in October 2009. The impact excavated material that confirmed the presence of water ice in permanently shadowed regions.

The Viking 1 lander touched down on Mars in July 1976 and operated until November 1982. It was the first spacecraft to successfully land on Mars and complete its mission. The lander remains on the Martian surface in the Chryse Planitia region.
